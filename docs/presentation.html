01 — Choosing the embedding

IMDB reviews are web text, so we pick a web-trained embedding

01Intuition

Reviews are informal web text

Literally NOTHING happens.

…a double MUST SEE one...!!!!

…this overrated crap…

…he has serious chops, IMHO.

The reviews are full of slang and messy punctuation, like forum posts.

02Tokenization

We split text to match the embedding's tokens

I didn't like it... 3/10!

remove HTML tags, lowercase,
split contractions & punctuation

ididn'tlikeit...3/10!

03Evidence

Almost every word has a vector

All words in the reviews

99.4%

Unique words

78.3%

The few words without a vector are mostly typos, symbols and rare names.

Embedding: GloVe 42B · 300 dimensions · trained on Common Crawl web text · best coverage of the embeddings we tested

02 — What the embedding captures

Embeddings know what words mean, but opposites look alike

Similarity

Nearest neighbours make sense

great→

prequelremaketrilogy

Closest words by cosine similarity: it ignores vector length.

Sentiment

Polarity can be read from the vectors

94.2%
± 0.6

A logistic regression reads a word's polarity from its GloVe vector, tested on 6,625 unseen words of the NLTK opinion lexicon.

Limitations

Opposites sit close together

0.77good ↔ bad
0.69love ↔ hate
0.65best ↔ worst
  • Embeddings assume words used in similar contexts have similar meanings (distributional hypothesis), so opposites, which share contexts, end up close.

Static vectors: “sick!” reads as “ill”  ·  No negation: “not good” is just “not” + “good”  ·  Unknown words: no vector, no signal

Both models fail when the words say the opposite of the score

agreement score = (pos − neg) / (pos + neg), from the NLTK opinion lexicon. Flipped for negative reviews: +1 = words match the label, −1 = words oppose

When the words go against the score, embed and pool barely beats guessing; the LSTM is better but still fails.
